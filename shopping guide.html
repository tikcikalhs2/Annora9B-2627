  Shopping Guide

Browse Products (Index):

Explore our homepage to discover the latest items. Use the category menu to find the clothes, mini dresses, or bags you are looking for.

Select & Add to Cart:

Click on a product, check the size chart and color options, then click "Add to Cart".

Checkout:

Open your shopping cart, verify your items, and click "Proceed to Checkout".

Enter Details & Choose Courier:

Fill in your complete shipping address and contact details, then select your preferred shipping carrier.

Make Payment:

Choose your payment method (Bank Transfer / E-Wallet / QRIS) and complete the payment according to the total amount.

Order Processing:

Once payment is confirmed, your order will be carefully packed and shipped to your doorstep.
